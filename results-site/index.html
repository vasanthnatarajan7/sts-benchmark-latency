Notes

  • Prism was the fastest on three of four clips and the slowest on clip B, where almost all of its 1.5s was turn detection.
  • Grok took about 3.5s on recording A1 in every run, but 0.96s on a second recording of the same sentence (A2). Lowering the volume or adding silence to A1 didn't change it. I haven't found the cause.
  • Gemini was the most consistent: all four clips within 88 ms of each other.
  • OpenAI's ~0.74s turn detection is its default end-of-speech window, which can be configured. I didn't tune anything for any model.
  • With 20 trials, p95 is close to the second-slowest trial.

Method

  • Direct WebSocket connections, no SDKs: Rime prism-v0, xAI grok-voice-3.0, Google gemini-3.8-live, OpenAI gpt-realtime-2.1.
  • Recordings streamed at real-time speed, 16 kHz mono PCM (resampled to 24 kHz for OpenAI, which requires it).
  • End of speech taken from the recording's audio level; the clock starts when that part of the audio was sent. Network time is included the same way for every model.
  • Fresh connection per trial, no retries. Failures, early replies and talk-overs are counted; all were zero.
  • One speaker, one machine and home network, one evening. Not measured: answer quality, voice quality, barge-in, phone-line audio.
  • No vendor sponsored or reviewed this test.
